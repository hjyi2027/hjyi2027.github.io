Director, AIscend Labs

I run the lab's AI research. Most of the 21 workshop papers are audits of what a benchmark or metric actually measures.

Selected papers, each a public PDF

  1. Accepted NeurIPS 2026, Creative AI track

    Restored lines: A model finds the poet’s hardest line breaks and declines to write them

    Hyunjun Yi

    A model recovers 70% of the breaks that strand a determiner or conjunction, yet writes them in its own free verse at 6.1% against the human 12.3%.

    PDFOpenReviewCode

  2. Accepted NeurIPS 2026, Creative AI track

    The dead ear: A language model’s inherited accent is an archive, not an author

    Hyunjun Yi

    The model treats rhymes lost to sound change as rhyme-like 40.0% of the time against a 1.2% floor, yet writes them at 0.6%.

    PDFOpenReviewCode

  3. Accepted NeurIPS 2026 workshops: Representations for the Physical Sciences; Physical Understanding for Decision-Making

    When does a Fourier neural operator fail? Spectral diagnostics for low-rank surrogates of reaction-diffusion

    Hyunjun Yi, David J. Zhang, Wenhao Lu, Jonathan Liu, Spursh Deshpande

    A compact operator’s one-step edge and its single-seed rollout advantage both reverse under three-seed averaging.

    PDFOpenReview

  4. Accepted WACV 2027 RWS workshop, oral and poster

    One in how many? False alert rates of live facial recognition in London, from controlled test to routine use

    Hyunjun Yi, Bryan Bu, June Young Kim, Raiyan Mazhar

    All 729 police deployments, 10.1 million recorded faces and 58 false alerts: the records cannot bound the rate the controlled test predicts.

    PDF

  5. Accepted WACV 2027 UniMUG workshop

    Is the synergy above the noise? Noise floors for understanding-generation claims in unified multimodal models

    Hyunjun Yi, Bryan Bu, June Young Kim, Raiyan Mazhar

    42% of 261 published synergy results sit inside their own noise floor, and no paper reports seed variation.

    PDF

  6. Accepted WACV 2027 AINIME workshop, oral

    Sharper is not closer: What no-reference metrics reward in anime restoration

    Bryan Bu, Hyunjun Yi, June Young Kim, Raiyan Mazhar

    MANIQA prefers a sharpened copy of the source frame to the frame itself in 90% of clips.

    PDF

9 open problems and questions settled and 7 records improved, listed in the open problems table, plus 2 new discoveries. Sole author of The 3-diametral number of Euclidean d-space, which answers a question of Bezdek and Lángi in every dimension, of Isosceles sets and two-distance sets in Euclidean space (arXiv:2611.05421), which settles Erdős’s 1946 question for fourteen new dimensions, and of uniqueness of the 277-point two-distance set in R23. With Bryan Zachary Bu and Karam Gill: t3(15) = 31, open since 1974, m(15) = 9 for Sylvester-Gallai, and g(9) = 45, open since 1997. Three of our papers are accepted for presentation at JMM 2027: the orchard paper, the Neumaier-graph paper, and Almost-equidistant sets, which I present. With Wyatt Choi: Complements in the lattice of topologies, which shows that Watson’s 1994 question about singular cardinals is independent of ZFC. Co-first author of A half-constant upper bound for Chowla’s cosine problem (arXiv:2610.17160).
