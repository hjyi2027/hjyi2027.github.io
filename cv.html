  1. Accepted NeurIPS 2026, Creative AI track

    Restored lines: A model finds the poet’s hardest line breaks and declines to write them

    Hyunjun Yi

    A model recovers 70% of the breaks that strand a determiner or conjunction, yet writes them in its own free verse at 6.1% against the human 12.3%.

  2. Accepted NeurIPS 2026, Creative AI track

    The dead ear: A language model’s inherited accent is an archive, not an author

    Hyunjun Yi

    The model treats rhymes lost to sound change as rhyme-like 40.0% of the time against a 1.2% floor, yet writes them at 0.6%.

    PDFOpenReviewCode

    Hyunjun Yi, David J. Zhang, Wenhao Lu, Jonathan Liu, Spursh Deshpande

Sole author of The 3-diametral number of Euclidean d-space, which answers a question of Bezdek and Lángi in every dimension, of Isosceles sets and two-distance sets in Euclidean space (arXiv:2611.05421), which settles Erdős’s 1946 question for fourteen new dimensions, and of uniqueness of the 277-point two-distance set in R23. With Wyatt Choi: Complements in the lattice of topologies, which shows that Watson’s 1994 question about singular cardinals is independent of ZFC. Co-first author of A half-constant upper bound for Chowla’s cosine problem (arXiv:2610.17160).
