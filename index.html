About

Hi! I'm Hyunjun, a senior at Seoul International School. I run AI research at AIscend. Most of my work asks the one question above. The decoding sampler. One swapped word in an instruction. Where a line breaks.

I also do mathematics, lately exact answers to old problems in discrete geometry, and I'm a competition director of YIMO, a free online math olympiad. Three of my papers were accepted to NeurIPS 2026. Every public result links to its paper or code.

William Carlos Williams, "The Red Wheelbarrow", 1923. Nothing in the sentence requires a break after "wheel". In Restored lines, a model recovers 70 percent of breaks like that one and writes them at half the human rate.

Toy layout for illustration: five source images, eight crops each, one fifth held out. The HRSID ship-detection benchmark was split by crop, and 94.8% of its test crops share pixels with a training crop. The audit is Half the test ships are in the training crops.
